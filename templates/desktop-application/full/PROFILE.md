# Desktop Application — Full

Use for a mature desktop product with broader compatibility, data, extension, release, security, or migration boundaries.

Full may document:

- architecture and major subsystems;
- project/file formats;
- plugins or scripting when they exist;
- update channels and portable mode when supported;
- data/config/cache locations;
- security, privacy, telemetry, or network behavior;
- packaging, signing, notarization, and release channels;
- backup/migration and upgrade compatibility;
- troubleshooting and diagnostics;
- development/testing and release workflow.

Every capability-dependent section is optional. Full means deeper documentation, not fabricated desktop features.
