# RepoForge Code of Conduct

RepoForge aims to maintain a professional, respectful, and inclusive environment for contributors, maintainers, users, and visitors.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully, including during technical disagreement;
- give constructive feedback and receive it in good faith;
- respect privacy, personal boundaries, and differences in background or experience;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and outcomes rather than people;
- help keep issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions useful and safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in RepoForge project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory conduct;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith;
- other conduct that a reasonable professional community would consider abusive or unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies to RepoForge-controlled project spaces and to people acting as official representatives of the project.

## Reporting

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the project maintainer using a private contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Do **not** open a public issue containing sensitive conduct reports, personal information, or details that could expose the reporter or affected people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers responsible for enforcement will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical. Depending on severity and context, responses may include clarification, a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Information from a report should be shared only with people who need it to review or resolve the concern, except where disclosure is required for safety or legal reasons.

## Related standards

This policy is original RepoForge text informed by widely used open-source community practices, including Contributor Covenant 3.0 and GitHub community-health guidance. Projects that need the verbatim Contributor Covenant should adopt the official policy and preserve its attribution and license terms.
