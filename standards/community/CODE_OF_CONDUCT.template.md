# Code of Conduct

## Our community standard

{{ project_name }} is committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive collaboration environment for contributors, maintainers, users, and other participants.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully, including when disagreeing;
- give constructive feedback and receive it in good faith;
- respect personal boundaries, privacy, and differing backgrounds or levels of experience;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and outcomes rather than people;
- help keep project spaces useful, safe, and welcoming.

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory conduct;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith;
- other conduct that a reasonable professional community would consider abusive or unsafe.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces and to people acting as official representatives of {{ project_name }}.

## Enforcement

Maintainers responsible for enforcement will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical. Depending on severity and context, responses may include clarification, a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report information should be shared only with people who need it to review or resolve the concern, except where disclosure is required for safety or legal reasons.
